Fed Chair Kevin Warsh left December's rate call open Wednesday, and stocks gave back some of their post-decision bounce. Innodata (INOD) fell another 3.1%, and Montana's Western District race keeps drawing national firepower with four days to go.

## In 60 seconds

- Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said "there's no firm conclusions now about monetary policy" on a December hike, even after holding rates at 3.75%-4.00% Wednesday.
- Innodata (INOD) fell 3.1% to $46.17, now down 31% over the past month, with Q3 earnings due November 5.
- Democrat Sam Forstag toured Flathead businesses as Republican Aaron Flint keeps bringing in national surrogates, four days before Montana's Western District vote.

## Montana & Bozeman

**Sam Forstag toured Flathead businesses Monday as Flint leaned on national Republicans for a fourth straight week.** Forstag stopped at Cutthroat Bagel Company in Whitefish and the SmartLam timber plant in Columbia Falls, where owners raised tariffs and falling Canadian tourism. Flint has now hosted President Trump in Kalispell, House Speaker Mike Johnson in Bozeman, and Vice President Vance in Belgrade within three weeks; Forstag called the parade of surrogates a sign Republicans see the same tightening polls he does. Three ratings outlets have moved the race toward him since mid-October, two to "toss up." Election Day is November 3. [Read more →](https://dailymontanan.com/?p=39747)
