Two big votes land this week: Israel chooses a new Knesset today, and the Fed's rate call comes Wednesday. Back home, Montana's Western District House race just flipped to a toss-up.

## In 60 seconds

- Israelis vote today in an election too close to call, while the Fed opens a two-day meeting with Wednesday's decision due at 2 p.m. ET.
- Stocks rose Monday: the Nasdaq added 0.82% to 26,169.38 and the Dow gained 0.28% to 52,361.58.
- Three election forecasters moved Montana's Western District House race to "toss up" this week, citing over $3.5 million in outside spending.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Israel votes today for a new Knesset**, the first national election since the Gaza war and a referendum on Prime Minister Netanyahu, who is standing trial on corruption charges. Recent polls show the newly formed Yashar party, led by former military chief Gadi Eisenkot, running neck-and-neck with Likud, with neither governing bloc clearly past the 61 seats needed. *Markets: a fractured result keeps Israel's next move on Iran, and the Hormuz oil-risk premium, unresolved until a coalition forms.* [Read more →](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/10/20/israels-elections-2026-explained-in-maps-and-charts)

## Montana & Bozeman

**Montana's Western District House race is now a toss-up.** Fox News, Cook Political Report and Inside Elections all shifted their ratings toward Democrat Sam Forstag this week, with Cook citing heavy spending by the Democratic House Majority PAC and Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC, over $3.5 million in one week alone. Forstag, a wildland firefighter, faces Republican Aaron Flint, a veteran and radio host who won the seat Ryan Zinke gave up. [Read more →](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/10/17/analysts-shift-western-montana-congressional-district-to-toss-up-status/)

**Gallatin County is bringing back mobile voter registration** ahead of the November 3 midterms, with officials noting about 83% of county voting is absentee, among the highest rates in the state. Montana has no early in-person voting, so officials are urging voters to mail or drop off ballots soon. [Read more →](https://nbcmontana.com/news/local/gallatin-co-brings-back-mobile-voter-registration-09-29-2026)
