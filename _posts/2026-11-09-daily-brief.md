## Politics & Geopolitics

**Alaska's Senate race is heading to a ranked-choice runoff.** Peltola leads Sullivan 102,962 to 102,325, a 637-vote gap, with neither clearing 50%. Tabulation starts November 18; both campaigns say it favors them. *Markets: Senate control already flipped to Democrats, so this race decides a margin, not a majority.* [Read more →](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/06/peltola-sullivan-campaigns-project-wins-while-quarter-ballots-are-outstanding/)

**Mississippi's Senate race is turning into a legal fight over ballots.** Hyde-Smith, trailing Colom by roughly 2,000 votes out of nearly a million cast, sent letters to all 82 counties demanding affidavit ballots be separated and preserved. Colom calls it an "unlawful attack on voter privacy." *Markets: a prolonged count in a state with no recount law raises the odds this ends up before a judge, or the Senate itself.* [Read more →](https://www.wlox.com/2026/11/06/colom-campaign-calls-hyde-smiths-affidavit-ballot-separation-preservation-demand-unlawful-attack-voter-privacy/)

**Israel's President Herzog still hasn't assigned the mandate to form a government.** Opposition leader Eisenkot holds 67 Knesset recommendations to Netanyahu's 46, but mutual vetoes left Herzog undecided as of his planned Sunday announcement. The new Knesset is sworn in Tuesday regardless. *Markets: a drawn-out coalition fight keeps Israeli assets under pressure into a second week.* [Read more →](https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/11/israeli-president-holds-back-mandate-despite-majority-for-eisenkot/)
