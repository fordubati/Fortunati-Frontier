The Fed's rate call comes at 2 p.m. ET today, the week's biggest event. Israel's election count still has no governing majority, and Innodata just lost a quarter of its value.

## In 60 seconds

- The Fed's rate decision lands today at 2 p.m. ET; markets price in roughly an 85% chance of a hold, with Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference to follow.
- Innodata (INOD) crashed 25.3% Tuesday to $44.74 after reports it cut about 550 jobs in India, with more cuts in the Philippines.
- Israel's vote count still leaves neither bloc at the 61-seat majority: Netanyahu's bloc holds 54, Eisenkot's 49, with roughly 600,000 soldier ballots still to come.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Israel's vote count still leaves no governing majority, with soldiers' ballots to come.** With about 84% of votes counted, Prime Minister Netanyahu's bloc holds 54 seats and former military chief Gadi Eisenkot's bloc holds 49, short of the 61 needed; Eisenkot's bloc plus the Arab parties' 17 seats would reach 66. Roughly 600,000 "double-envelope" ballots, many from soldiers, may not be fully counted until Friday. *Markets: a drawn-out count delays any Israeli move on Iran and keeps the Hormuz oil-risk premium in place.* [Read more →](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-28-2026/)
