## Politics & Geopolitics

**Scott Colom (D) won Mississippi's U.S. Senate race, and Cindy Hyde-Smith (R) conceded Wednesday evening**, after Hinds County's affidavit ballots widened his lead to roughly 4,400 votes. He's the first Democrat Mississippi has sent to the Senate since 1982, in a seat forecasters rated safely Republican. *Markets: a flipped deep-red seat widens the Senate's new Democratic majority beyond expectations, raising the odds of gridlock on fiscal legislation next year.* [Read more →](https://www.mississippifreepress.org/liveblog-mississippis-2026-u-s-senate-election-results/)

**Israel's 26th Knesset was sworn in Monday, and Gadi Eisenkot said coalition talks have made "significant progress."** He's seeking a mandate to form a government built around four principles, including designating Hamas a terrorist group it must disarm. Netanyahu's bloc, at 53 seats, is 8 short of a majority. *Markets: a faster, more stable coalition would ease the uncertainty weighing on Israeli assets; a prolonged fight extends it.* [Read more →](https://www.dailysabah.com/world/mid-east/eisenkot-reports-progress-in-israeli-government-coalition-talks/amp)

## Montana & Bozeman

**Montana State, the city of Bozeman and NASA are running a new weather station at the headwaters of Bozeman Creek,** which began operating this fall. It tracks snow depth, snowpack water content and precipitation, and will also help NASA calibrate its NISAR satellite. Bozeman Creek supplies about 40% of the city's water, so the data will feed drought planning as snowmelt timing shifts.
