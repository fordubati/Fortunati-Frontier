Yields kept climbing overnight, oil backed off Monday's spike, and BlackBerry was the watchlist's biggest mover.

## In 60 seconds

- The 10-year Treasury yield hit about 5.24%, its highest since 2007, pushing the 30-year mortgage rate to a one-year high of 7.37%.
- Oil eased Tuesday morning after Monday's Iran-driven spike, with Brent falling back to about $97.51 from over $101.
- The U.S. and China finalized a $60 billion tariff-cut deal; a court challenge to Trump's tariff authority gets a hearing Wednesday.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-09-29/markets.png)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to roughly 5.24% Monday, its highest since 2007; the 30-year hit about 5.56%, its highest since 2004 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-september-28-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080420627.html)).

**Stocks**: The Dow fell 0.67% to 51,481.51, the S&P 500 dropped 0.77% to 7,683.69, and the Nasdaq slid 0.92% to 26,820.38 Monday on rising yields and oil volatility. Boeing dropped nearly 7% after the FAA delayed 737 MAX 10 certification over a new software glitch ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-september-28-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080420627.html)).

**Housing**: The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate rose to 7.373% Tuesday, a one-year high and a fourth straight daily increase ([Forbes Advisor](https://forbes.com/advisor/mortgages/mortgage-rates-09-29-26)).

**Tariffs**: The U.S. and China locked in a "30-for-30" deal cutting tariffs on $60 billion of combined trade, plus a pledge for China to buy 10 million metric tons of U.S. coal a year in 2027-28 ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/china-us-tariff-cuts-60-billion-trump-xi-rcna600214)). Separately, a court hears a challenge Wednesday to whether Trump's Section 301 tariffs exceed what Congress authorized ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/25/economy/tariffs-trump-section-301-case)).

**Commodities**: Brent crude fell to about $97.51 a barrel Tuesday, down from over $101 Monday, while gold slid to roughly $4,150/oz, its lowest since August 5, on bets on further Fed hikes ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-price-today-tuesday-september-29-2026-gold-opens-near-2-month-low-but-brent-crude-also-moving-lower-104439751.html)).

## Watchlist

![Watchlist]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-09-29/watchlist.png)

**BB** +7.2%: BlackBerry kept climbing on last week's fiscal Q2 beat, revenue up 26% year-over-year, with QNX posting record revenue and its largest-ever design win, worth $100 million in future royalties ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/blackberry-jumps-4-record-qnx-170716630.html)).

**INOD** -3.5%: Innodata gave back more of last week's Meta "Muse" speculation-driven rally, though shares remain up about 18% over the past month. Rahul Singhal becomes CEO Wednesday.

Quiet: JPM, GBCI.

## World

**Japan**: The yen weakened toward 158/dollar Monday before settling near 157.26 Tuesday, as a stronger dollar and rising U.S. yields outweighed Tokyo's warnings ([TradingEconomics](https://tradingeconomics.com/japan/currency)).

**China**: Beijing's State Council pledged new measures to counter a slowing economy and stabilize its property market, as forecasters expect the factory PMI to rebound to 50.1 in September ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-28/china-studies-policies-to-boost-economy-steady-housing-market)).

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Iran & the Strait of Hormuz**: The standoff over Trump's rejected Hormuz proposal kept driving oil, though Tuesday's pullback in Brent suggests some of Monday's shock premium is fading ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/28/oil-prices-surge-after-trump-rejects-irans-plan-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz)). *Markets:* this remains the swing factor behind this week's moves in oil, gold, and yields above.

**U.S. politics**: Trump announced a planned $15 billion steel mill in Iowa with Mesabi Metallics, promising 2,000 jobs, while Senate Majority Leader John Thune said taxpayer money shouldn't fund political ads like the White House's current one ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/live-blog/trump-congress-2026-midterm-elections-ai-iran-war-live-updates-rcna600167)). *Markets:* signals continued industrial-policy spending ahead of the midterms.

## One question

Oil spiked Monday and eased by Tuesday morning without the strait actually reopening. How much of that "risk premium" is real supply fear, and how much is just habit?
