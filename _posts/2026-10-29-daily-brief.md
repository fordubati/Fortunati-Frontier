The Fed held rates Wednesday in a split 10-2 vote. Israel's election remains deadlocked with roughly 480,000 ballots still to count, and Innodata bounced back a day after its layoff-driven crash.

## In 60 seconds

- The Fed held its rate at 3.75%-4.00% Wednesday in a 10-2 vote; Governors Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack dissented, wanting a quarter-point hike instead.
- Israel's election stays deadlocked with about 90% counted: Netanyahu's bloc has 53 seats, Eisenkot's 48, Arab parties 19; about 480,000 soldier and diplomat ballots won't be final until November 4.
- Innodata (INOD) rebounded 6.5% to $47.65 Tuesday, a day after crashing 25% on layoff reports, though shares were down almost 4% premarket Wednesday.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-29/markets.png)

**The Fed held its target rate at 3.75%-4.00% Wednesday, a 10-2 vote.** Governors Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack dissented, wanting a quarter-point hike; the statement called activity "strong" with inflation "elevated." Stocks rose into the decision: the S&P 500 added 0.28% to 7,743.98 and the Nasdaq gained 0.27% to 26,549.85. The 10-year yield eased to 4.99%, down 13 bp on the week. December 8-9 is the next meeting, with fresh projections. [Read more →](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a1.htm)

**The 30-year mortgage rate held near 7.32%, even as pending home sales hit a record low.** Redfin says pending sales fell for the four weeks ended October 18, down 14.4% year over year, as 7.4% rates pushed the median payment near an all-time high. ## Politics & Geopolitics

**Israel's election stays deadlocked, with soldiers' ballots still to come.** With about 90% counted, Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 seats and Gadi Eisenkot's opposition bloc 48, with Arab parties at 19; none reaches 61. About 480,000 troop and diplomat ballots, worth roughly 12 seats, won't be final until November 4, and Eisenkot has reportedly ruled out serving under Netanyahu. *Markets: a drawn-out coalition fight keeps the Hormuz oil-risk premium in place.* [Read more →](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913122)
