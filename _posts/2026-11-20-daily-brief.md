The Nasdaq is down nearly 10% this month as AI-stock jitters spread into bank shares, with every watchlist ticker off at least 4% this week. Mortgage rates reversed course and jumped back toward 7.3%. Alaska's Senate race has been called for Democrat Mary Peltola, but a federal appeal could still complicate her Nov. 25 certification.

## In 60 seconds

- The Nasdaq closed at 23,879.20 Thursday, down 5.9% on the week and 9.6% on the month, as AI-stock worries and a leaderless Nvidia keep dragging on tech.
- Bank stocks joined the slide: Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan are each down more than 6% this week on fears that slowing AI spending could hit the broader economy.
- The 30-year mortgage rate jumped 17 bp to 7.28%, erasing last week's dip, while Alaska's Senate race awaits Nov. 25 certification the Trump DOJ is still trying to appeal.

## New York

**The federal government that has spent two years fighting NYC's congestion pricing program just invited other cities to apply for the same kind of toll**, via $100 million in new DOT grants; MTA chair Janno Lieber called it "a pretty remarkable about-face." The toll has kept vehicle entries into Manhattan's congestion zone under 500,000 a month all year except October.
