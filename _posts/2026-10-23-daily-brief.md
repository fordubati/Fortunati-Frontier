The Nasdaq led a broad pullback Thursday, BlackBerry erased most of its rally with no clear reason given, and Glacier Bancorp posted a record quarter.

## In 60 seconds

- The Nasdaq fell 2.6% to 26,008.86 Thursday while the Dow held flat at 52,867.98.
- BlackBerry gave back 8.7% to $8.59, unwinding most of the week's QNX-driven rally.
- Two more tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz this week, off Oman and Iraq, as WTI crude holds near $108.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-23/markets.png)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield rose 1 bp to 5.13%, still down 6 bp over the past week. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, speaking at IMF meetings in Bangkok last week, said hotter-than-expected inflation hasn't changed his view of the economy ahead of the October 27-28 FOMC meeting. [Read more →](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/treasury-yields-soaring-thats-not-082600984.html)

**Stocks**: The Nasdaq fell 2.6% to 26,008.86, now down 2.64% over the past week. The Dow was flat at 52,867.98 (+0.02%) and the S&P 500 is up 0.58% on the day.

**Housing**: The 30-year fixed mortgage rate eased 8 bp to 7.32%, its first pullback after weeks of increases tied to the bond selloff.

**Commodities**: WTI crude sits at $107.89, up 13.1% over the past week, as the Strait of Hormuz attack pattern runs into a third week.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Hormuz attacks continue**: Two more tankers were hit this week. The Kuwaiti-flagged Al Dasma caught fire after a projectile strike near Oman on Wednesday, and the Greek-owned Soyia was hit off Iraq on Tuesday, the first strike there in over a month. Crews were unhurt in both. A Washington Institute count puts October's attacks above 28, though the pace has slowed from the month's first half. *Markets: oil holds near $108 on the ongoing risk premium, with mortgage and pump-price effects still working through.* [Read more →](https://seatrade-maritime.com/security/fire-on-kotc-vessel-struck-off-oman-tanker-hit-near-iraq)
