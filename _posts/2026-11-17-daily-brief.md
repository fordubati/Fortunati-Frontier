Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO and a new U.S. rule restricting AI-chip exports sent the Nasdaq into correction territory Monday. Nvidia reports earnings after today's close, Trump and Xi meet in Shenzhen this week, and Israel's coalition talks and Alaska's Senate tabulation both hit key moments Wednesday.

## In 60 seconds

- The S&P 500 fell 1.92% Monday to 7,416.59 and the Nasdaq dropped 2.49% to 24,509.76, now over 10% below its October record, after Jensen Huang's exit as Nvidia CEO and a new U.S. curb on AI-chip exports.
- Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter earnings after today's close, with revenue expected near $109 billion; CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO.
- Futures odds of a quarter-point Fed rate hike on Dec. 8-9 remain near 100% after last week's hot inflation print, with the 10-year Treasury yield at 5.15%.
