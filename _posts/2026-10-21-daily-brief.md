Stocks edged higher, yields ticked up again, and BlackBerry was the watchlist's standout on a record royalty backlog.

## In 60 seconds

- The Dow rose 1.02% to 52,495.88 Tuesday; the 10-year Treasury yield rose 6 bp to about 5.15%.
- BlackBerry jumped 8.7% to $8.87 after QNX's royalty backlog hit a record $1.2 billion, up over $250 million in two quarters.
- The Fed's October 27-28 meeting is next week, the first with Kevin Warsh as chair since his October 16 IMF remarks in Bangkok.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-21/markets.png)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield rose 6 bp to about 5.15%, still down 9 bp over the past week. The 30-year climbed 5 bp to 5.48%. Markets are pricing an October pause and watching for signals from new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh ahead of the October 27-28 FOMC meeting ([Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/calendar-update-wsjs-timiraos-posts-fed-chairman-kevin-warsh-is-scheduled-to-appear-in-a-moderated-conversation-at-the-fall-meetings-of-the-international-monetary-fund-on-oct-16-in-bangkok-1130-pm-us-eastern-time-on-oct-15)).

**Stocks**: The Dow rose 1.02% to 52,495.88 and the S&P 500 added 0.28% to 7,712.57. The Nasdaq was roughly flat (-0.02%) at 26,424.82, still down 2.87% over the past week after last week's tech-led selloff.

**Housing**: The 30-year fixed mortgage rate held near 7.40% as of October 15, up 64 bp over the past month on the bond selloff ([Zillow via Mortgage Reports](https://themortgagereports.com/94666/mortgage-and-refinance-rates-today-august-17-2022)).

**Commodities**: WTI crude sat near $101.91, up 7.25% over the past week on lingering Strait of Hormuz risk premium.
