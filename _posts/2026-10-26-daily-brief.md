## Politics & Geopolitics

**Israel votes Tuesday in its first national election since the Gaza war, a referendum on Prime Minister Netanyahu as he stands trial on corruption charges.** One recent poll had the new Yashar party, led by former military chief Gadi Eisenkot, narrowly ahead of Likud, 22 seats to 21, with neither bloc near the 61 seats needed to govern. *Markets: a fractured result leaves Israel's next move on the Iran strike Trump reportedly delayed, and the Hormuz risk premium under oil, unresolved.* [Read more →](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/10/20/israels-elections-2026-explained-in-maps-and-charts)
