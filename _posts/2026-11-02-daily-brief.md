Montana's Western District race and the full U.S. midterms hit the polls Tuesday, with forecasters still favoring Democrats for the House. Fed December hike odds jumped to 85.6% after last week's hold, a sign traders now expect a rate increase, not a cut. And Netanyahu's allies are floating a unity government with rival Gadi Eisenkot as Israel's coalition talks drag on.

## In 60 seconds

- Montana's Western District House race and the full U.S. midterms vote Tuesday; Decision Desk HQ gives Democrats a 79% chance at the House and 57% at the Senate.
- Fed December hike odds climbed to 85.6% on CME FedWatch Thursday, up from 78.3% the day before, after the Fed's 10-2 hold last Wednesday.
- Netanyahu's allies are floating a national unity government with challenger Gadi Eisenkot, with Trump reportedly receptive, since neither bloc holds the 61 seats needed to govern alone.
