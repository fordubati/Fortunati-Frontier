Markets head into the week near Friday's levels after last week's hot inflation print. Israel's opposition cleared a key hurdle over the weekend toward replacing Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, and two big events land Wednesday: Alaska's ranked-choice Senate tabulation and a Trump-Xi meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.

## In 60 seconds

- The S&P 500 closed Friday at 7,561.67, up 2.51% on the week, with the 10-year Treasury yield holding near 5.10% as traders price a roughly 91% chance of a Fed rate hike at the Dec. 8-9 meeting.
- Avigdor Liberman dropped his longstanding veto on including ultra-Orthodox parties in a coalition over the weekend, a breakthrough for Gadi Eisenkot's bid to form Israel's next government without Netanyahu.
- Alaska's Senate race (Peltola vs. Sullivan) heads to a ranked-choice tabulation Wednesday, the same day Trump and Xi are set to meet at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Avigdor Liberman dropped his veto on bringing ultra-Orthodox parties into a coalition with Gadi Eisenkot over the weekend**, conditioned on those parties giving up support from Arab lawmakers. United Torah Judaism still wants a freeze on seminary-student conscription before joining, the main sticking point left in talks. *Markets: a workable Eisenkot coalition would end months of political uncertainty that has kept Israeli assets under a cloud.* [Read more →](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/s1xaucyagl)

**Alaska's Senate race between Democrat Mary Peltola and Republican Dan Sullivan heads to a ranked-choice tabulation Wednesday**, since neither topped 50% of first-choice votes. Election officials have been counting overseas ballots, which Alaska accepts for up to 15 days after the election.
