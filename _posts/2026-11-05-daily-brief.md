Stocks staged their best session in months Wednesday, reversing an overnight futures slide once investors digested a Democratic Congress split from a Republican White House. Bank and software shares led the bounce without any stock-specific news behind it. Innodata reports third-quarter results after today's close, its first since last month's reported layoffs.

## In 60 seconds

- The Dow Jones jumped 3.03% Wednesday to 53,356, its best session in months; the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.44% to 25,737.
- Two Senate races remain uncalled: Alaska is too close to call, and Mississippi was separated by roughly 28 votes with 97% counted, though Democrats have already clinched both chambers.
- Innodata (INOD) reports Q3 earnings after today's close, its first since last month's reported layoffs; shares are up 3.3% Wednesday but still down 9.1% on the week.

## Markets

Futures had fallen overnight on worries a Democratic Congress slows AI spending, but shares reversed once investors concluded the split between Congress and the White House was already priced in.
