Wall Street futures are pointing sharply higher this morning, with Nvidia up 9% premarket after President Trump suggested on social media that the board could bring Jensen Huang back as CEO. It's the first clear bounce after a week that pushed the Nasdaq into correction territory. In Montana, a nonprofit has stepped in to defend the voter-approved "Montana Plan" after the Attorney General said he wouldn't.

## In 60 seconds

- Nvidia jumped 8.8% premarket after Trump floated rehiring ousted CEO Jensen Huang; Nasdaq-100 futures rose 3.4%, S&P 500 futures 2.7%, and Dow futures 2%.
- Nvidia's board has reportedly resisted a Huang return, and interim CEO Colette Kress remains in place for now.
- In Montana, the Transparent Election Initiative is intervening to defend the "Montana Plan" corporate-spending ban, adding former U.S. Solicitor General Seth Waxman to its legal team.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-11-23/markets.png)

**Nvidia rose 8.8% in premarket trading Monday after President Trump posted that he'd be open to Nvidia's board rehiring Jensen Huang**, who resigned as CEO on Nov. 12. The move helped lift Nasdaq-100 futures 3.4%, S&P 500 futures 2.7% and Dow futures 2%, a rebound from a week that drove the Nasdaq into its worst stretch since 2020. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/22/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**Nvidia's board has reportedly told associates it isn't considering Huang's return**, even as Trump's comments moved the stock; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has separately urged the board to look outside the company for its next permanent chief. Colette Kress stays on as interim CEO. [Read more →](https://thenextweb.com/news/trump-backs-jensen-huangs-return-as-nvidia-board-reportedly-says-no)

**Other AI-linked names rallied alongside Nvidia:** Alphabet rose 6%, AMD 4.8%, CoreWeave 8%, and recent IPO Anthropic 4.4% in premarket trading, as traders bet last week's selloff had gone too far. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-nvidia-shares.html)

## Montana & Bozeman

**The Transparent Election Initiative, the nonprofit behind the voter-approved "Montana Plan," is intervening in federal court to defend the corporate-spending ban after Attorney General Austin Knudsen said he wouldn't.** The group added former U.S. Solicitor General Seth Waxman to its legal team. The challenge, filed Nov. 4 by the Institute for Free Speech on behalf of the Montana Republican Party and several conservative groups, argues the ban conflicts with *Citizens United*; a ruling on a preliminary injunction could come as soon as mid-December, and the attorney behind it says he's prepared to take the fight to the U.S. Supreme Court.
