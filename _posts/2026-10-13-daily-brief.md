Bank earnings season opened with a split verdict Tuesday: JPMorgan missed on revenue, Goldman Sachs beat, and two watchlist names gave back their recent gains.

## In 60 seconds

- JPMorgan's revenue missed Wall Street's estimate despite 6% profit growth, a rare miss; Goldman Sachs beat with a 23% revenue jump.
- INOD fell 3.43% and BB fell 5.16% Monday, pulling back from recent rallies with no fresh company news behind either move.
- September CPI lands Wednesday, the last major data point before the Fed's October 28-29 meeting.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-13/markets.png)

**Stocks**: Monday was mixed. The S&P 500 slipped 0.06% to 7,807.24, the Nasdaq fell 0.44% to 27,245.90, and the Dow rose 0.38% to 51,850.55.

**Banks**: JPMorgan kicked off earnings season Tuesday with net income of $15.3 billion (up 6% from a year ago) and diluted EPS of $5.57, but revenue of $50.2 billion fell short of the $50.85 billion analysts expected. Return on equity held at 17%, same as a year ago. Goldman Sachs beat, posting $18.68 billion in revenue (up 23%) and EPS of $17.01. Citigroup and Wells Fargo also reported Tuesday. [Read more →](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000019617/000162828026065582/a3q26erfex992supplement.htm)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield is 5.22%, down 2 bp on the week but up 42 bp on the month, with CPI due Wednesday ahead of the Fed's October 28-29 meeting.

**Housing**: The 30-year mortgage rate held at 7.40%, roughly a four-year high, unchanged from last week.

**Commodities**: WTI crude is $96.24/bbl, flat on the week but up 3.83% on the month.
