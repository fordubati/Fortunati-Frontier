Oil fell to its lowest level in weeks as OPEC+ held output steady. Innodata kept sliding even as one analyst stayed bullish, and Mississippi's 82 counties face today's deadline to certify Scott Colom's win.

## In 60 seconds

- WTI crude fell to $89.62 a barrel, its lowest level in about two months, as OPEC+ held December output at September's level amid oversupply worries.
- Innodata (INOD) fell another 4.9% Thursday to $36.29, down 6% this week; Craig-Hallum cut its price target to $75 from $120 but kept a Buy rating.
- Mississippi's 82 counties face a Friday deadline to certify Scott Colom's Senate win, two days after Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded.

## Montana & Bozeman

**Bozeman's city commission unanimously raised its cash-in-lieu-of-water-rights fee from $6,000 to $13,050 per acre-foot**, a 117.5% jump that lifts the typical new single-family home's fee from about $1,400 to $3,000 a year. The fee hadn't moved in 18 years; officials cited post-pandemic growth and underpriced water. It applies only to new development and takes effect March 1 instead of staff's proposed Nov. 23, giving developers who hadn't followed the issue time to lock in the old rate. [Read more →](https://www.explorebigsky.com/city-unanimously-oks-water-fee-hike/175628)
