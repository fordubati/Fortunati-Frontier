Thursday was a broad rally: the Dow led with a 1.08% gain, and the watchlist's two beaten-down names, INOD and BB, both bounced hard after days of selling. Oil stayed elevated near $102 a barrel as the Strait of Hormuz disruption drags on.

## In 60 seconds

- The Dow rose 1.08% to 52,177.69, its best day in weeks; the S&P 500 added 0.33% and the Nasdaq edged up 0.08%.
- INOD jumped 6.45% to $56.57 and BB rose 5.71% to $8.70, both rebounding after recent slides with no fresh company news behind the bounce.
- WTI crude held near $101.91/bbl, up 7.25% this week, as fighting around the Strait of Hormuz keeps supply uncertain.
