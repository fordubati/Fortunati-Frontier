Thursday's rally reversed hard: the Nasdaq fell into correction as investors dumped chip and AI stocks, erasing Wednesday's post-election bounce. Innodata beat big after the close, but the stock had already fallen with everything else.

## In 60 seconds

- The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.52% Thursday to 24,315, entering correction (down 10%+ from its recent high) as chip stocks led a broad AI-selloff.
- Innodata (INOD) reported Q3 revenue up 53% to $95.6 million and raised full-year guidance, but the stock had already dropped 10.8% that day with the market.
- Alaska's Senate race remains too close to call two days after polls closed; Peltola led Sullivan by roughly 600 votes as of Wednesday morning.
