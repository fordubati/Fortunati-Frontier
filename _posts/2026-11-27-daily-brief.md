Wall Street is back today for a Black Friday half day after Thursday's Thanksgiving closure, trading until 1 p.m. ET. Markets enter the session close to Wednesday's levels, still digesting this week's hot inflation reading.

## In 60 seconds

- Wall Street reopens today for an abbreviated Black Friday session, closing at 1 p.m. ET, after Thursday's holiday.
- Entering the session, the S&P 500 sits at 7,201 (down 1.9% this week) and the 10-year Treasury yield at 4.91% (down 19 bp), with a December Fed rate hike still the consensus bet.
- A Manhattan judge upheld Mayor Mamdani's two-year rent freeze for rent-stabilized apartments, rejecting a landlord lawsuit.

## New York

**A Manhattan State Supreme Court judge upheld Mayor Mamdani's two-year rent freeze for rent-stabilized apartments, dismissing a landlord group's lawsuit.** Judge Brendan Lantry ruled the Rent Guidelines Board's freeze "has a rational basis and must therefore be upheld as valid," rejecting claims the board manipulated income data to justify it.
