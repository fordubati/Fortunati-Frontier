Nvidia topped Wall Street's estimates late Tuesday and raised its outlook, a bright spot after a rough week for tech stocks. Trump met Xi in Shenzhen ahead of Thursday's unprecedented three-way sit-down with Putin. Alaska's Senate race enters ranked-choice tabulation today, and Israel's opposition is piecing together a coalition without Netanyahu.

## In 60 seconds

- Nvidia's fiscal third-quarter revenue hit $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago, beating the $108.8 billion Wall Street expected; shares rose in after-hours trading.
- Trump and Xi met in Shenzhen ahead of Thursday's first-ever trilateral summit with Putin, billed as nuclear-arms talks; the White House says Ukraine isn't on the agenda, Russia says it is.
- Alaska's ranked-choice Senate tabulation begins today with Mary Peltola near 50% of first-choice votes and 96% counted; certification is targeted for Nov. 25.

## World

**Xi personally greeted Trump at the airport in Shenzhen**, a rare gesture ahead of Thursday's first-ever three-way summit with Russia's Putin, billed around reducing nuclear arsenals. The White House says Ukraine isn't on the agenda; a Kremlin aide says the talks will continue toward a negotiated settlement there, and Kyiv's foreign minister warned the summit could "encourage Putin to continue this war."
