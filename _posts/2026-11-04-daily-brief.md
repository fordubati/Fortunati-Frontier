Democrats swept control of Congress overnight, flipping seven Senate seats in a bigger rout than forecasters expected. Montana's Western District flipped too, with Democrat Sam Forstag beating Republican Aaron Flint to end two years of an all-Republican delegation. Stock futures fell as investors weighed what a Democratic Congress means for the AI-spending boom.

## In 60 seconds

- Democrats won control of the House and Senate overnight, flipping seven Senate seats for their widest House majority since 1993.
- Montana's Western District flipped: Forstag led Flint 108,776 to 96,638 with 78% counted; Flint conceded, though the AP hadn't called the race as of early Wednesday.
- Stock futures fell on the result: S&P 500 futures dropped more than 0.5%, Nasdaq 100 futures fell as much as 2.5%, on worries a divided government slows the AI buildout.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Democrats swept control of Congress overnight, flipping seven Senate seats in a bigger rout than forecasters expected.** NBC projects at least 258 House seats for Democrats, their widest majority since 1993, with Hakeem Jeffries poised to become the first Black Speaker. Senate flips include Maine, Texas, Kansas (the state's first Democratic senator since 1932), Iowa, Ohio, North Carolina, and South Carolina. *Markets: stock futures fell on the size of the win, with Nasdaq 100 futures down as much as 2.5% on worries a Democratic Congress slows AI and data-center investment.* [Read more →](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/live-blog/midterm-election-results-2026-news-trump-live-updates-rcna605763)

## Montana & Bozeman

**Montana flipped its Western District House seat for the first time in 20 years.** Democrat Sam Forstag led Republican Aaron Flint 108,776 to 96,638 with 78% counted; Flint called Forstag to concede, though the AP hadn't made the race official as of early Wednesday. Montana's Senate seat stayed Republican, with Kurt Alme holding it in the four-way race.
