Polls open today for the full U.S. midterms and Montana's toss-up Western District race. Markets are still digesting last week's AI-spending jitters, and Innodata's slide deepened to 31% on the week after reports of mass layoffs.

## In 60 seconds

- Election Day: all 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats, and Montana's razor-close Western District race go to voters today; first results trickle in after 6 p.m. Eastern (4 p.m. Mountain).
- Innodata (INOD) fell another 5.7% Monday to $41.32, extending its weekly slide to 31% after reports of roughly 550 layoffs in India and the Philippines.
- The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.94%, down 6 bp on the week, as stocks stayed choppy following last week's AI-spending scare.
