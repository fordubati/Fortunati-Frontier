Monday's rally reversed last week's AI rout: the Nasdaq jumped 2.74% as chipmakers bounced back. Nvidia missed the party, falling after its board opened an investigation into CEO Jensen Huang. In Israel, Herzog handed Eisenkot the mandate to form a government, bypassing Netanyahu.

## In 60 seconds

- The Nasdaq rose 2.74% to 25,253.14 Monday, and the Dow gained 2.15% to 52,389.75, as AMD and other chipmakers bounced back from last week's selloff.
- Nvidia fell about 4% after its board launched a probe into CEO Jensen Huang following his admission of an affair with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly ties to Chinese intelligence.
- Israel's President Herzog gave rival Gadi Eisenkot the mandate to form a government, passing over Netanyahu; Eisenkot has 28 days (plus a possible 14-day extension) to build a coalition.

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Mississippi's Senate race is still uncalled: Colom leads Hyde-Smith by about 2,500 votes** out of roughly 925,000 cast. An estimated 12,000-20,000 affidavit ballots remain, and both sides are preparing for a legal fight over which ones count. *Markets: a contested count in a seat Republicans expected to hold easily adds to uncertainty around the Senate's new, tighter math.* [Read more →](https://mississippitoday.org/2026/11/05/thousands-of-affidavit-ballots-outstanding-in-mississippi-senate-election-campaigns-prepare-for-legal-fight/)

**Alaska's Senate race remains a tossup: Peltola led Sullivan by fewer than 700 votes as of Friday, with 74% counted.** Neither has cleared 50%, so it heads to a ranked-choice tabulation November 18. *Markets: Senate control already flipped, so this seat decides the majority's size, not its existence.* [Read more →](https://www.kptv.com/2026/11/07/alaska-senate-race-remains-undecided-days-after-election-day/)

**Israel's President Herzog gave Gadi Eisenkot, not Netanyahu, the mandate to form the next government.** Eisenkot drew 67 lawmaker recommendations to Netanyahu's 46, though Netanyahu's bloc holds more seats (53 to 48). Eisenkot has ruled out governing with Arab-led parties, so he needs both ultra-Orthodox parties to reach 61. *Markets: a prolonged coalition fight, with Netanyahu staying on as caretaker PM, extends the uncertainty weighing on Israeli assets.* [Read more →](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/israels-president-picks-eisenkot-over-netanyahu-to-form-next-government)
