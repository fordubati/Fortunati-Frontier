Trump, Xi and Putin wrapped an unprecedented trilateral summit in Shenzhen with a "peace declaration" that never mentions Ukraine or Iran. The AP has called Alaska's Senate race for Democrat Mary Peltola after Wednesday's ranked-choice count. Stocks were mixed as the Nasdaq edged up while Innodata extended its monthly slide.

## In 60 seconds

- Trump, Xi and Putin held an 80-minute trilateral in Shenzhen; Chinese state media's four-point "peace declaration" covers UN reform, fuel supply and nuclear dialogue but omits Ukraine, Iran, Taiwan and North Korea entirely.
- The AP has called Alaska's U.S. Senate race for Democrat Mary Peltola, who closed Wednesday's ranked-choice tabulation with 50.1% of first-choice votes; certification is set for Nov. 25.
- The Nasdaq rose 0.42% to 24,429.88 Wednesday while the S&P 500 and Dow slipped, as futures price roughly 90% odds of a quarter-point Fed hike on Dec. 9.

## World

**Trump, Xi and Putin ended their trilateral in Shenzhen after about 80 minutes, with no statements from the three leaders.** Chinese state media later published a four-point "peace declaration" backing UN reform, more refined-fuel supply, and dialogue on nuclear risk. The text never mentions Ukraine, Iran, Taiwan or North Korea. Trump called his Ukraine discussion with Putin "long"; Putin said only "we have shared interests in Ukraine," and Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the war wasn't "the central topic." *Markets: an inconclusive summit leaves Ukraine-linked sanctions and oil-supply risk largely unresolved.* [Read more →](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/19/us-and-china-want-to-help-end-ukraine-war-trump-says-ahead-of-trilateral-with-russia)
