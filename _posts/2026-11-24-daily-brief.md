Nvidia closed up 31% Monday after its board confirmed Jensen Huang's return as CEO, driving the Nasdaq and S&P 500 to their best day since April 2025. Futures gave some of it back overnight, and Anthropic shares briefly fell below their IPO price.

## In 60 seconds

- Nvidia closed up 31% Monday on Jensen Huang's confirmed return as CEO, adding about $952 billion in value and lifting the Nasdaq 6.5% and S&P 500 4.7%, both indexes' best day since April 2025.
- Futures turned lower overnight into Tuesday: Nvidia fell more than 3% premarket, and Anthropic dropped over 4%, briefly trading below its IPO price.
- Mortgage rates rose to 7.28% last week, reversing a four-week slide, while the 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.90% ahead of Wednesday's PCE inflation report.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-11-24/markets.png)

**Nvidia closed up 31% Monday after its board confirmed Jensen Huang would return as CEO**, days after President Trump floated the idea on social media. The stock added about $952 billion in market value in a single session, from $3.025 trillion to $3.978 trillion, on a record options day of more than 11 million contracts. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**The rally lifted the Nasdaq 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.7%, both their best day since April 9, 2025, and the Dow rose 3.01% to 49,595.31.** Tesla rose 11.46%, Meta 6.55% and Alphabet 6.13%, as the "Magnificent Seven" added roughly $1.65 trillion in combined value. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**Futures turned lower overnight into Tuesday: Nasdaq-100 futures fell 2%, Dow futures dropped 259 points and S&P 500 futures fell 1%.** Nvidia slipped more than 3% premarket, Micron fell 4.3%, and Anthropic dropped over 4%, briefly trading below its IPO price. Biotech Insmed jumped 14% after Sanofi agreed to buy it for $41 billion, or $175 a share in cash. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**The 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.90% early Tuesday, little changed, with traders pricing a quarter-point Fed rate hike as highly likely at the Dec. 8-9 meeting.** The PCE price index, the Fed's last inflation read before that decision, is due Wednesday. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**The 30-year mortgage rate rose to 7.28% last week, up 17 bp and reversing a four-week decline.** Economists now say a drop below 6% looks unlikely anytime soon. [Read more →](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/11/19/a-tough-backdrop-for-mortgage-rates-as-holiday-slowdown-begins)

## New York

**New York City has billed more than $500 million under its new pied-à-terre tax on second homes valued above $5 million, Mayor Mamdani said, though that's less than half of the roughly 17,000 homeowners the city targeted.** The city has granted at least 8,900 exemption requests so far. [Read more →](https://www.6sqft.com/mamdani-says-nyc-billed-500m-under-pied-a-terre-tax-despite-thousands-of-exemptions/)

**City Council members are pushing Speaker Julie Menin to bring the long-stalled Delivery Protection Act to a vote**, after an Amazon delivery partner laid off about 200 e-bike workers this month. The bill would require Amazon to directly employ delivery drivers instead of using subcontractors.
