Bank earnings season split further Wednesday: Bank of America's profit fell even as revenue rose, Morgan Stanley beat, and the week's biggest number, September CPI, lands this morning.

## In 60 seconds

- Bank of America's Q3 net income fell 17% to $6.93 billion despite revenue rising 3%, as expenses jumped; Morgan Stanley beat, with net revenue up 12% to $20.3 billion.
- September CPI releases at 8:30am ET today, the last major inflation read before the Fed's October 28-29 meeting.
- INOD extended its slide to a fourth session, down 11.9% this week with no fresh company news; BB, JPM, and GBCI are quiet.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-14/markets.png)

**Stocks**: Tuesday was mixed and modest. The S&P 500 slipped 0.02% to 7,805.45, the Nasdaq fell 0.15% to 27,204.38, and the Dow dropped 0.35% to 51,670.10.

**Banks**: Bank of America's revenue rose 3% to $29.96 billion, but net income fell 17% to $6.93 billion and diluted EPS dropped to $0.91 from $1.04, as noninterest expense climbed to $19.72 billion from $17.34 billion. Morgan Stanley beat estimates: net revenue hit $20.3 billion (up 12% from a year ago), diluted EPS rose to $3.03 from $2.80, and return on equity held near 18%. [Read more →](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000895421/000089542126000288/a3q26msearningsrelease.htm)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield is 5.24%, up 2 bp on the day but down 4 bp on the week. September CPI is due at 8:30am ET, two weeks ahead of the Fed's next meeting.

**Housing**: The 30-year mortgage rate is 7.40%, up 12 bp on the week to roughly a four-year high. The next housing-starts report lands October 20.

**Commodities**: WTI crude is $96.24/bbl, roughly flat on the week.
