Markets are in a holding pattern ahead of this morning's jobs report, mortgage rates sit near a three-year high, and Innodata rode an Accenture-driven AI rally Thursday.

## In 60 seconds

- The 10-year Treasury yield held near 5.24% and the 30-year near 5.62% Friday as traders awaited the nonfarm payrolls report; markets price a 72% chance the Fed holds rates steady this month.
- Innodata jumped 9.3% Thursday to $71.85, catching a lift from Accenture's strong results, which signaled robust enterprise demand for AI and data services.
- The 30-year fixed mortgage rate is 7.28%, up 25 bp on the week and near its highest in three years.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-02/markets.png)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield is 5.24%, little changed Friday; the 2-year sits at 4.79% and the 30-year at 5.62%, just off its highest level since 2004. Traders are watching today's jobs report, with markets pricing a 72% chance the Fed holds rates steady in October ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/02/treasury-yields-bonds-nonfarm-payrolls.html)).

**Stocks**: The S&P 500 rose 0.19% to 7,666.45, the Nasdaq added 0.04% to 26,871.60, and the Dow inched up 0.04% to 50,926.56 Thursday as yields retreated from multiyear highs. All three are down on the week (S&P -0.49%, Dow -0.82%, Nasdaq -0.25%) ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)).

**Housing**: The 30-year fixed mortgage rate is 7.28%, up 25 bp on the week and 62 bp on the month, as the broader bond selloff keeps borrowing costs elevated ([Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates)).

**Commodities**: WTI crude is $96.16/bbl, down 3.2% on the day but still up 13.7% on the month.

## Montana & Bozeman

**Community**: Requests to the Bozeman School District's Warm Winter Drive have surged to about 330 students this year, up from roughly 50 when the program started two years ago. Donations of coats, boots, and snow pants are accepted through October at school offices and the Dry Fly Saloon ([Explore Big Sky](https://www.explorebigsky.com/bozeman-students-show-increased-need-for-winter-clothing/165378)).

## One question

Yields have been grinding toward two-decade highs for weeks on an oversupply of government debt, not just inflation fears. If today's jobs report comes in weak, does that actually bring yields down, or does a weaker economy with this much debt to refinance just add another reason for yields to stay high?
