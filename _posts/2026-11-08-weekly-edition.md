Democrats swept Congress Tuesday, flipping Montana's Western District for the first time in 20 years, and stocks fell on the size of the win. A Wednesday relief rally gave way to Thursday's AI-stock rout, pushing the Nasdaq into correction before a partial Friday bounce. The Bank of England surprised with a hike to 4%, citing Middle East-driven inflation risk. Innodata posted a blowout quarter the market barely noticed.

## The week in 60 seconds

- Democrats won control of the House and Senate Tuesday, flipping seven Senate seats and Montana's Western District, where Sam Forstag beat Aaron Flint 132,003 to 122,694.
- The Nasdaq fell 5.52% Thursday to 24,315, its worst day since April 2025, before bouncing 1.1% Friday; the week's AI-bubble debate intensified.
- Innodata (INOD) reported record Q3 revenue of $95.6 million, up 53%, and raised guidance, but shares still fell 15.5% on the week.
- The Bank of England raised its rate to 4.00% Thursday, an 8-1 vote, as the Fed's December hike odds climbed to 85.6%.
- China's rare-earth export-control suspension expires Monday, two days before Bozeman's new city charter cleared and before Israel's coalition talks remain stuck.

### Bonds

**Yields fell across the curve as the midterms removed an uncertainty and traders leaned toward a Fed hike.** The 10-year fell 14 bp to 4.84%, down 43 bp this month; the 2-year fell 15 bp to 4.46%. Fed December hike odds jumped to 85.6% on CME FedWatch, up from 78.3%, after last week's 10-2 hold. [Read more →](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/interest-rates-hike-odds-december-2026-jump-85-after-feds-recent-decision/)

**The Bank of England raised its rate to 4.00% Thursday in an 8-1 vote**, with one member wanting a half-point move to 4.25%. The bank cited inflation risk partly tied to the Israel-Iran conflict pushing up energy costs. [Read more →](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-report/2026/November-2026)

### Stocks

**Stocks swung wildly after the election.** Futures fell Tuesday night on the size of the Democratic sweep, reversed into a 3.03% Dow rally Wednesday, then crashed Thursday: the Nasdaq fell 5.52% to 24,315, its worst session since April 2025, entering correction. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index plunged 12% and the VIX spiked over 65% intraday. Stocks partly recovered Friday: Nasdaq +1.1%, S&P +0.3%, Dow -0.5%. [Read more →](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-5-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-plunge-chip-stocks-melt-down-193726491.html)

**The AI-bubble debate sharpened.** Goldman Sachs strategist Ryan Hammond warned investor enthusiasm may be nearing its peak, while a CFA Institute analysis published Wednesday said the evidence it reviewed leaned more bullish than bearish on the trade. [Read more →](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/blogs/enterprising-investor/2026/i-tried-to-prove-ai-was-a-bubble)

### Housing

**Mortgage rates eased for a third straight week.** Freddie Mac's 30-year average fell to 7.18% for the week ended November 5, helped by the post-election bond rally. [Read more →](https://www.rismedia.com/2026/11/05/mortgage-rates-edge-down-third-straight-week/)

**Housing demand stayed weak regardless.** Redfin data showed pending home sales at their lowest level in records dating to 2021, with one tracker citing an October drop of 10.5% year over year. [Read more →](https://realestatenews.com/2026/11/05/mortgage-rates-fall-again-but-buyer-activity-stays-muted)

### Tariffs & Trade

**China's rare-earth export-control suspension expires Monday, November 10,** a week before the APEC summit in Shenzhen. Analysts widely expect a negotiated extension rather than a lapse, though licensing for heavy rare earths would persist either way. A separate U.S. pause on sanctions against Chinese chip-tied firms lapses Sunday, a day earlier. [Read more →](https://skillings.net/rare-earths-truce-chinas-november-10-expiry-risk-explained/)

### Commodities

**Oil stayed soft even as Hormuz attacks continued.** WTI crude sits at $99.64/bbl, down 3.61% on the week. The Joint Maritime Information Center logged six attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz since November 1, pushing its regional threat level to "Severe." Minutes from the Bank of Japan's September meeting, released this week, showed a 7-2 vote to raise its rate to 1.25%, with some members pushing for a faster pace. *Markets: a Reuters poll expects the BOJ to hike again to 1.50% in December.* [Read more →](https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/361056120/asian-shares-skid-after-ai-worries-send-wall-street-tumbling)

### United Kingdom

The Bank of England's hike to 4.00% caught markets off guard; many expected a hold. *Markets: it narrows the government's room to maneuver ahead of the November 26 Autumn Budget.* [Read more →](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-report/2026/November-2026)

## Politics & Geopolitics

### Conflicts

**Attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz continued this week**, with the Joint Maritime Information Center recording six incidents since November 1, including a tanker crew member killed November 6. The pace keeps breaking the interim U.S.-Iran truce from mid-June. [Read more →](https://gcaptain.com/new-wave-of-attacks-hits-tankers-and-lng-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz-and-persian-gulf/)

### U.S. National Politics

**Democrats won control of both chambers Tuesday**, flipping seven Senate seats for their widest House majority since 1993; Hakeem Jeffries is positioned to become the first Black Speaker. Two Senate races stayed uncalled at midweek: Alaska, where Democrat Mary Peltola led Republican Dan Sullivan by roughly 600-700 votes while the Justice Department disputed a similarly named candidate, and Mississippi, where Democrat Scott Colom led incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith by 1,145 votes with a provisional-ballot deadline of November 10. Senate control is already decided regardless. [Read more →](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/democrats-win-sizable-us-house-majority-amid-sweep-close-races)

### Global Affairs

**Israel's coalition talks stayed deadlocked.** Netanyahu pushed a national unity government with election rival Gadi Eisenkot, but Eisenkot publicly rejected it, calling Netanyahu "unfit to lead this nation and certainly in no position to lecture on unity." Opposition leaders instead backed Eisenkot on November 3 to form a rival "national emergency government," while Yisrael Beytenu's Avigdor Liberman has ruled out joining any Netanyahu-led coalition. [Read more →](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/3/israeli-opposition-leaders-back-eisenkot-to-oust-netanyahu)

## Montana & Bozeman

[Read more →](https://www.kbzk.com/news/local-news/lets-break-down-the-2026-gallatin-valley-housing-report)

### Business

Kalispell-based Glacier Bancorp, the state's largest bank holding company, posted record quarterly net income of $104.6 million, up 54% from a year earlier, in results filed November 3. [Read more →](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000868671/000086867126000074/gbci-09302026xex991.htm)

### Local Politics

**Bozeman voters approved an amended city charter Tuesday**, expanding the City Commission from four members to six plus a mayor, requiring mandatory geographic wards drawn by a neutral commission, and letting a directly elected mayor serve a full four-year term instead of two years as deputy mayor. A companion question on whether ward commissioners should be elected citywide or only within their ward was narrowly leading toward ward-only voting in unofficial results. The changes phase in starting with the 2029 city election. [Read more →](https://nbcmontana.com/news/local/bozeman-voters-approve-changes-that-reshape-city-government)

**Montana's Western District flipped for the first time in 20 years.** The Associated Press called the race for Democrat Sam Forstag at 10:02 a.m. Thursday, with a final tally of 132,003 votes to Republican Aaron Flint's 122,694. [Read more →](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/11/05/ap-makes-montana-race-call-for-forstag/)

## Connecting the dots

- The midterms, billed as the week's biggest risk, turned out to be a sideshow: the real move came Thursday from AI-stock fatigue, not Washington.
- Yields fell even as the Fed leans toward a December hike and the Bank of England hiked, a reminder bond-market calm can outweigh central-bank signals in the short run.
- Oil stayed soft despite continued Hormuz attacks and a looming China rare-earth deadline, a sign markets are watching AI valuations more closely than geopolitical risk this week.

## Questions to think about

- If Thursday's Nasdaq correction was profit-taking after a hot run rather than a bubble popping, what would Monday's trading need to look like to tell the difference?
- Innodata beat earnings by a wide margin and still fell on the week. How much of a company's stock price is now just a bet on the market's mood toward AI, separate from its own numbers?
- Bozeman's new charter won't take effect until 2029. How should voters weigh a structural change whose first real test is three years away?

## Interview angle

**"Why did Innodata's stock fall even after a 53% revenue beat?"** The report landed Thursday after the close, the same day the broader market fell into correction; the stock's move tracked the index more than the company's own numbers.

**"What does a Democratic Congress mean for markets near-term?"** Futures fell on the size of the win over AI and data-center regulation fears, but stocks reversed within a day once investors concluded a split government was already priced in.

**"Why did the Bank of England hike into a possible slowdown?"** It cited inflation risk tied partly to Middle East conflict pushing up energy costs, even with a tightening Autumn Budget due later this month.
