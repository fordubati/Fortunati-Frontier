Wall Street closed the books on September with yields still elevated, Trump's tariff authority back in court, and BlackBerry the watchlist's standout.

## In 60 seconds

- The Dow fell 0.9% to 50,906.05 Wednesday on strong growth data that kept yields high; the Nasdaq eked out a 0.2% gain to end a winning September.
- The U.S. Court of International Trade heard a challenge Wednesday to Trump's forced-labor tariffs on goods from 60 countries, including the EU and China.
- BlackBerry jumped on record QNX revenue and a new Alloy Kore design win worth over $100 million in future royalties.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-01/markets.png)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield is 5.26%, up 2 bp on the day and 30 bp on the week; the 2-year sits at 4.89%, the 30-year at 5.59% ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/treasury-yields-bonds-selloff.html)).

**Stocks**: The Dow fell 0.86% to 50,906.05, the S&P 500 dropped 0.25% to 7,651.54, and the Nasdaq rose 0.24% to 26,861.06 Wednesday. For September, the Nasdaq gained 1.9% while the Dow lost 4.3% ([AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/major-us-stock-indexes-fared-wednesday-9302026-136901112)).

**Housing**: The 30-year fixed mortgage rate is 7.03%, up 8 bp on the week and 38 bp on the month, near a three-year high ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/current-mortgage-rates-10-01-2026/)).

**Tariffs**: The Court of International Trade heard arguments Wednesday on tariffs against 60 trading partners Trump accuses of tolerating forced-labor imports. Small businesses and 25 Democratic-led states argue the tariffs exceed presidential authority, the same authority the Supreme Court narrowed in February ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/30/economy/trumps-tariffs-are-back-in-court-again)).

**Commodities**: WTI crude is $96.16/bbl, down 3.2% on the day but up 13.7% on the month; gold held near $4,160/oz ([Forbes Advisor](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/investing/oil-prices-today/)).

## World

**Japan**: The yen fell 0.5% to about 158.21/dollar, the worst G-10 performer Wednesday, after a Bank of Japan summary disappointed investors hoping for clearer signals on more rate hikes beyond September's move to 1.25% ([GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9104635/japanese-yen-weakens-as-bojs-meeting-summary-disappoints-investors)).

**China**: The official factory PMI rose to 50.1 in September from 49.8, returning to growth after a two-month contraction, helped by new mortgage-interest subsidies and stronger high-tech output ([AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/business/2026/09/30/factory-activity-returns-to-expansion-in-a-positive-sign-for-chinas-economy/)).

## Politics & Geopolitics

**Tariff authority on trial**: Wednesday's hearing tests how far Trump can stretch tariff powers after February's Supreme Court ruling narrowed them. A loss would unwind duties on goods from 60 countries ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/30/economy/trumps-tariffs-are-back-in-court-again)). *Markets:* a ruling against the tariffs could ease import costs but add fresh policy uncertainty for trade-exposed sectors.

**Iran talks stall**: Qatari mediator Ali al-Thawadi made little progress this week pushing Iran and the U.S. toward a deal, after meetings with Iran's foreign minister and U.S. envoy Jared Kushner ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-30-2026/)). *Markets:* a stalled deal keeps a floor under oil's war premium.

## Montana & Bozeman

**Housing**: The Regional Housing Coalition released "Bridging the Gap," a report laying out funding options state and local leaders could use to finance more affordable housing across Montana ([Montana Right Now](https://www.montanarightnow.com/bozeman/new-bozeman-report-outlines-ways-to-fund-affordable-housing-statewide/article_e7485b8b-0e71-413c-bdbf-d523a691c376.html)).

**Wildlife**: Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks euthanized 14 black bears at a residence north of Bozeman after finding the animals had grown dependent on human food and too comfortable around people ([Mtn News / The Dugout](https://mtnewsthedugout.substack.com/p/investigation-at-residence-near-bozeman)).

## One question

A court could strike down tariffs on 60 countries within weeks. If it does, how much of this year's inflation and yield story unwinds with it, versus staying baked in from spending already locked in?
