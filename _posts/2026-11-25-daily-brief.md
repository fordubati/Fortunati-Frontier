Stocks are giving back some of Monday's Nvidia-fueled rally, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq each down about 2.4% this week. The PCE inflation report is due today, the last word before a likely December rate hike.

## In 60 seconds

- The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are down about 2.4% this week even after Monday's Nvidia-fueled rally; the PCE inflation report is due today, the last read before the Dec. 8-9 Fed meeting where a rate hike still looks likely.
- Treasury yields fell across the curve this week, the 10-year down 20 bp to 4.92%, while the 30-year mortgage rate climbed to 7.28% and pending home sales fell to their lowest level on record.
- Montana Republicans and business groups sued within hours of voters passing Initiative 194, the state's new ban on corporate dark money in elections; the attorney general says he won't defend it in court.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-11-25/markets.png)

**The S&P 500 is at 7,237, down 2.4% this week, and the Nasdaq at 23,734, down 2.5%, even after Monday's single-day gains of 4.7% and 6.5% on Nvidia's rally.** Tuesday was mixed: the Dow rose 0.85% to 50,019 while the Nasdaq slipped 1.5%. [Read more →](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**The PCE price index, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, is due out today, the last read before the Dec. 8-9 meeting where a rate hike still looks likely.** Oil is helping the case for cooler inflation: WTI crude is at $89.53 a barrel, down 17.3% this month. Treasury yields fell anyway this week, with the 10-year down 20 bp to 4.92%. [Read more →](https://www.oxfordeconomics.com/resource/us-pce-nowcast-broadening-pressures-lock-in-december-rate-hike/)

**The 30-year mortgage rate rose to 7.28% last week, and pending home sales fell to their lowest level in Redfin's records, which date to 2021.** Redfin calls it the strongest buyer's market since 2013, with more than half a million more sellers than buyers nationwide. [Read more →](https://www.mpamag.com/us/mortgage-industry/market-updates/mortgage-rates-rise-again-as-home-prices-slip-buyers-gain-leverage/598114)

## Montana & Bozeman

**Montana Republicans and business groups sued to block Initiative 194, the state's new ban on corporate political spending, within hours of voters approving it by about 65%.** Attorney General Austin Knudsen says the measure is unconstitutional and won't defend it in court; the case is consolidated before a federal judge in Great Falls. [Read more →](https://dailymontanan.com/?p=40077)

## New York

**Federal prosecutors charged a longtime Cravath, Swaine & Moore staffer with insider trading, alleging he used confidential client information, including about Paramount's merger with Warner Bros., to pocket at least $70,000 since April 2025.** Chetram Balli, a processing specialist at the Midtown firm since 1989, faces up to 20 years in prison if convicted; Cravath itself isn't accused of wrongdoing. [Read more →](https://www.amny.com/law/staffer-midtown-law-firm-insider-trading/)
