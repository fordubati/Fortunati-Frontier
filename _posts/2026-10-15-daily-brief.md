Wednesday's hot CPI print sent stocks to their worst day in weeks, and the selloff hit the watchlist hard: INOD fell another 9%, now down 16% in a week.

## In 60 seconds

- September CPI rose 3.7% year over year (core 2.6%), hotter than the recent trend; the S&P 500 fell 1.11% and the Nasdaq dropped 1.88%, their worst day in weeks.
- INOD fell 8.98% to $53.14, extending its slide to a fifth session and down 16.2% this week with no fresh company news.
- Treasury yields actually eased despite the hot inflation read: the 10-year fell 3 bp to 5.21%, down 6 bp on the week.

## Markets

![Markets, last 3 months]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/charts/2026-10-15/markets.png)

**Stocks**: Wednesday was the market's worst session in weeks. The S&P 500 fell 1.11% to 7,718.94, the Nasdaq dropped 1.88% to 26,692.87, and the Dow slipped 0.09% to 51,621.48.

**Inflation**: September CPI rose 3.7% year over year and 0.6% month over month, both above the recent pace. Core CPI (strips out food and energy) rose 2.6% year over year. The next CPI print lands November 10, two weeks after the Fed's October 28-29 meeting. [Read more →](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)

**Bonds**: The 10-year Treasury yield is 5.21%, down 3 bp on the day and 6 bp on the week, even as the hotter CPI print landed. The 2-year yield held near 4.79%.

**Commodities**: WTI crude is $96.24/bbl, roughly flat on the week.
