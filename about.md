# About the Frontier

**The Fortunati Frontier** is a morning brief on markets, politics, and Montana, written from Bozeman.

## What you get

- **Daily Brief (Mon–Sat, 7 AM Mountain):** a five-minute skim of what moved and why it matters.
- **Weekly Edition (Sundays, 7 AM Mountain):** the full picture, in the same order every week:
  1. **Markets:** bonds, stocks, housing, tariffs & trade, commodities
  2. **Key economies:** Japan, the United Kingdom, China
  3. **Politics & geopolitics:** conflicts, U.S. national politics, global affairs
  4. **Montana & Bozeman:** housing, local business, local politics

## How it's written

- **Neutral and factual.** What happened, what each side argues, and why it matters for markets and business.
- **Connected.** Stories are cross-referenced when one event drives another.
- **Thought-provoking.** Each edition ends with questions worth sitting with.
- **Sourced.** Key facts link to their sources.

## Who writes it

Rohan Fortunati, a Finance student (Economics and Accounting minors) at Montana State University, with research and drafting assistance from Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The Frontier started as a personal training habit: read the world every morning like a financier would.

*Educational commentary only. Nothing here is investment, legal, or financial advice.*
